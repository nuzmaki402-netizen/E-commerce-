Our Journey

A Legacy of Innovation

Key milestones that shaped the world's favorite beverage.

1886

The Beginning

Dr. John Pemberton creates the original formula in Atlanta, Georgia. A legend is born.

1915

Iconic Bottle

The contour bottle design is patented, creating one of the most recognized shapes in history.

1928

Olympic Partner

First Olympic Games sponsorship begins a tradition of supporting global sports excellence.

1982

Diet Coke Launch

Introduction of Diet Coke revolutionizes the beverage industry with zero-calorie options.

Today

Global Empire

Serving over 1.9 billion drinks daily across 200+ countries worldwide.
